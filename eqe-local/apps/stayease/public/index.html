About StayEase

StayEase helps you find and book a hotel in a few simple steps. Pick where you want to go and your dates, compare hotels and rooms, and book in minutes.

What you can do
  • Search hotels by city and dates
  • See hotel details, rooms and prices
  • Book a room with your details
  • Find your booking with your confirmation number and last name
Good to know

This is a demo website. Hotels, prices and bookings are made up. No real booking is made and no payment is taken, so please don't enter real card details.
